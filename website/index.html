The common ground

One plot is only the beginning.

A peak efficiency result means little without the array, periphery, precision, dataflow, technology node, and accuracy conditions that produced it. This benchmark keeps those conditions visible.

  1. 01
    Compose

    Describe the complete IMC datapath through canonical components.

  2. 02
    Normalize

    Translate reported results into comparable 1-bit operation metrics.

  3. 03
    Interpret

    Read efficiency, density, scale-up, and accuracy as a coupled system.
