SRAM charge- and current-domain model inputs

Match: QS → QS-Arch, QR → QR-Arch, QS-QR → compute memory, IS → current summing.

Noise and energy follow Gonugondla et al. Table III with the Table II 65 nm parameters (σVt = 23.8 mV, α = 1.8, κ = 0.08 fF0.5, W·L·Cox = 0.31 fF) and uniform, independent operand bits. The paper’s array is 512 rows with CBL = 270 fF. The ADC SQNR is computed exactly for the digitized signal at its SQNR-optimal clipping range. Gonugondla et al., TCAD 2022 ↗

Resistive eNVM model inputs

The published surface models current-summing ReRAM, MRAM, and FeFET columns with a six-bit ADC and a fixed 20 ns read period, for N from 16 to 5,000. Other ADC precisions use the source’s N = 64 ADC sweep (ReRAM and MRAM only). Behavioral model calibrated against a 22 nm MRAM prototype. Roy & Shanbhag, JXCDC 2024 ↗

SRAM QR Monte Carlo cross-check

Browser port of Kavishwar and Shanbhag’s 28 nm capacitor-mismatch and ADC-transfer simulation at 0.9 V, using the unit capacitance above. It reports compute SNR for a full-range ADC and, at N = 64, 128, or 256, for the tuned ADC window of the paper’s sweeps. The paper sweeps 0.01–0.30 fF and 0.1–1.6 mV. Kavishwar & Shanbhag, TCAS-I ↗

ASiM network-accuracy screening
%

Shown for analog SRAM choices. Accuracy is read from ASiM’s published result tables (Figs. 6, 7, 9, and 18): 256 rows, W8A8 checkpoints, and noise swept from 0 to 0.2% Vpp at an 8-bit ADC. Other settings are translated or bounded, and the result says which. ASiM models a generic charge-domain column validated on CR-CIM silicon; it does not distinguish QS, QR, and QS-QR. Zhang et al., TVLSI 2025 ↗
