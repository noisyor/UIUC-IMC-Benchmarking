Resistive eNVM model inputs

The published surface models current-summing MRAM, ReRAM, and FeFET arrays with a six-bit ADC. Other ADC settings are energy-only extrapolations.

SRAM QR accuracy-model inputs

This deterministic browser simulation follows the repository’s 28 nm QR capacitance-mismatch and ADC-transfer model. It estimates compute SNR, not energy or area.

A measured SNRcol is needed for accuracy-aware comparison. ADC bits alone do not establish compute accuracy.
