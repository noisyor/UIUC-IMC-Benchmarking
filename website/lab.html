Resistive eNVM model inputs

The published surface models current-summing MRAM, ReRAM, and FeFET arrays with a six-bit ADC. Other ADC settings are energy-only extrapolations. Roy & Shanbhag, JXCDC 2024 ↗

SRAM QR accuracy-model inputs

This deterministic browser simulation follows the repository’s 28 nm QR capacitance-mismatch and ADC-transfer model. It estimates compute SNR, not energy or area. Kavishwar & Shanbhag, TCAS-I ↗
