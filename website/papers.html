Publications and literature coverage

Methodology papers and benchmark literature through 2024.

The CICC paper introduces the benchmarking framework, and the OJ-SSCS article extends its data and analysis. The accompanying paper collection currently spans seven publication years, from 2018 through 2024.

2022IEEE CICC

Comprehending In-memory Computing Trends via Proper Benchmarking

Naresh R. Shanbhag and Saion K. Roy · 2022 IEEE Custom Integrated Circuits Conference · pp. 1–7 · Invited paper

The conference paper establishes the compositional view and explains why full-stack diversity, the energy-efficiency versus compute-SNR trade-off, and inconsistent reporting make IMC progress hard to judge.

Read via DOI ↗Open artifact ↗
N. R. Shanbhag and S. K. Roy, “Comprehending In-memory Computing Trends via Proper Benchmarking,” 2022 IEEE Custom Integrated Circuits Conference (CICC), pp. 1–7, 2022. doi: 10.1109/CICC53496.2022.9772817.
2022IEEE OJ-SSCS

Benchmarking In-Memory Computing Architectures

Naresh R. Shanbhag and Saion K. Roy · IEEE Open Journal of the Solid-State Circuits Society · vol. 2 · pp. 288–300 · Open access

The journal article applies the framework to more than 70 IC designs published since 2018, compares SRAM and emerging-memory IMCs with digital accelerators, distinguishes bank and processor behavior, and argues for direct compute-accuracy reporting.
